How Biweekly Payments Work

Paying half your mortgage every two weeks results in 26 half-payments per year, equivalent to 13 full monthly payments instead of 12. That extra payment goes directly to principal.

Why It Saves Money

  • One extra payment per year reduces principal faster
  • Less principal = less interest charged
  • Compound effect accelerates over time
  • Typical 30-year loan paid off in ~25 years

Implementation Options

Lender program: Some lenders offer official biweekly plans. DIY method: Make 1/12 extra principal payment each month. Biweekly service: Third-party services (often charge fees - usually not worth it).
