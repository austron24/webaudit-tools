Tipping Guidelines

Standard tipping practices in the United States:

  • Restaurants: 15-20% of pre-tax bill
  • Excellent Service: 20-25%
  • Average Service: 15-18%
  • Takeout: 10-15% (optional)
  • Bars: $1-2 per drink or 15-20% of tab
