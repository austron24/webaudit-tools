Buy Extra: Always purchase 10-15% more paint than calculated to account for touch-ups and mistakes.

Two Coats: Two thin coats provide better coverage and durability than one thick coat.
🎯
Dark Colors: Darker colors may require 3+ coats, especially over light colors.
📏
Primer: Use primer for new drywall, stains, or drastic color changes for best results.
