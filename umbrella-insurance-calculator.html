💡 Why Umbrella Insurance?

Umbrella insurance provides extra liability coverage beyond your home and auto policies. It protects your assets if you're sued for:
  • Auto accidents causing serious injury
  • Injuries on your property (slip and fall)
  • Dog bites
  • Defamation/libel claims
  • Landlord liability
