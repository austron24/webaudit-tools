What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple calculation using your height and weight to estimate body fat. It's widely used as a screening tool to identify potential weight problems in adults.

How BMI is Calculated

BMI is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in meters. The formula is: BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)²

BMI Categories

  • Underweight (BMI < 18.5): May indicate malnutrition, eating disorders, or other health issues.
  • Normal weight (BMI 18.5-24.9): Generally considered a healthy weight range.
  • Overweight (BMI 25-29.9): May indicate excess body weight and increased health risks.
  • Obese (BMI ≥ 30): Indicates significantly excess body weight with higher health risks.

Limitations of BMI

While BMI is a useful screening tool, it doesn't directly measure body fat. Athletes with high muscle mass may have a high BMI without excess fat. For a complete health assessment, consult with a healthcare professional.
