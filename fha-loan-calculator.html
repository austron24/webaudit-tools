💡 FHA Mortgage Insurance (MIP)

Upfront MIP: 1.75% of the loan amount, added to your loan balance

Annual MIP: 0.55% of the loan balance for loans with LTV > 90%

Duration: For loans with less than 10% down, MIP lasts the life of the loan. For 10%+ down, MIP can be removed after 11 years.
