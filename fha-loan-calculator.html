Understanding FHA Loans

FHA loans are government-backed mortgages designed for borrowers with lower credit scores or smaller down payments.

FHA Loan Requirements

  • Minimum down payment: 3.5% (with 580+ credit score)
  • Credit score: 500-579 requires 10% down; 580+ requires 3.5%
  • DTI ratio: Generally up to 43%, sometimes higher
  • Property: Must be primary residence

FHA Mortgage Insurance (MIP)

  • Upfront MIP: 1.75% of the base loan amount
  • Annual MIP: 0.55% for 30-year loans over 15 years
  • Duration: Life of loan if down payment < 10%; 11 years if ≥ 10%
