Understanding Property Taxes

Property taxes are local taxes based on the value of your property. They fund schools, roads, emergency services, and local government.

Key Concepts

  • Assessed Value: The value assigned by your local assessor (may differ from market value)
  • Assessment Ratio: Percentage of market value used for taxes (varies by state)
  • Mill Rate: Tax per $1,000 of assessed value
  • Homestead Exemption: Reduction for primary residences in many states
