Understanding Cost of Living

Cost of living indexes compare the relative expense of living in different cities. A higher index means higher costs.

  • Index of 100: Represents the average US city
  • Housing: Typically the largest factor (30-50% of the index)
  • Other Factors: Food, transportation, healthcare, utilities, taxes
  • State Taxes: No income tax in TX, FL, WA, NV, WY, SD, AK, TN, NH
  • Remote Work: Consider keeping high salary while living in lower-cost area
