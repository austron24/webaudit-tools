About Unix Timestamps

Unix timestamp (or Epoch time) is the number of seconds that have elapsed since January 1, 1970, 00:00:00 UTC. It's widely used in programming to represent dates and times in a simple, timezone-independent format. The Year 2038 problem occurs when 32-bit systems overflow after 03:14:07 UTC on January 19, 2038.
