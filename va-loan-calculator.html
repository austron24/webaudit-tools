Understanding VA Loans

VA loans are mortgages guaranteed by the Department of Veterans Affairs, available to veterans, active service members, and eligible surviving spouses.

VA Loan Benefits

  • No down payment required in most cases
  • No PMI (private mortgage insurance)
  • Competitive interest rates
  • Limited closing costs
  • No prepayment penalties

VA Funding Fee Rates (2024)

  • First use, 0% down: 2.15% (Regular), 2.40% (Reserves)
  • First use, 5%+ down: 1.50% (Regular), 1.75% (Reserves)
  • Subsequent use, 0% down: 3.30% (all)
  • Exempt: Veterans with disability rating, Purple Heart recipients
