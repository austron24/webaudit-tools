About Tax Refunds

Your tax refund is the difference between what you paid in taxes (through withholding) and what you actually owe. This calculator provides an estimate based on 2024 tax brackets.

  • Standard Deduction (2024): $14,600 (single), $29,200 (married filing jointly)
  • Child Tax Credit: Up to $2,000 per qualifying child under 17
  • SALT Deduction Cap: $10,000 limit on state and local tax deduction
  • Medical Expenses: Deductible if exceeding 7.5% of AGI
  • Filing Deadline: April 15 (or next business day)
