Understanding Title Insurance

Title insurance protects against losses from defects in the title that existed before you bought the property, such as liens, encumbrances, or ownership disputes.

Two Types of Policies

  • Owner's Policy: Protects the buyer (one-time premium, lasts as long as you own the property)
  • Lender's Policy: Protects the mortgage lender (required by most lenders)

Who Pays?

Varies by state and local custom. In some areas the seller pays for owner's policy, in others the buyer pays. Lender's policy is typically paid by the buyer.
