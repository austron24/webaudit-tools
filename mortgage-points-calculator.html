What Are Mortgage Points?

Mortgage points (or discount points) are upfront fees you pay to reduce your interest rate. One point equals 1% of your loan amount and typically reduces your rate by 0.25%.

When Points Make Sense

  • You plan to stay in the home past the break-even point
  • You have extra cash at closing
  • You want to maximize tax deductions (points may be deductible)
  • Current rates are high and you want to lock in savings

When to Skip Points

If you might refinance or sell before the break-even point, points usually don't pay off. The average homeowner moves or refinances every 7-10 years.
