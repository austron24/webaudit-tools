Understanding Debt-to-Income Ratio

Your DTI ratio is a key metric lenders use to evaluate your ability to manage monthly payments and repay debts.

DTI Guidelines

  • Below 20%: Excellent - Strong financial position
  • 20-36%: Good - Healthy debt level
  • 36-43%: Fair - May limit borrowing options
  • 43-50%: High - Difficult to get approved for new credit
  • Above 50%: Too High - Financial stress likely
