Understanding DTI Ratios

Debt-to-income ratio is a key factor lenders use to evaluate mortgage and loan applications.

  • Below 36%: Excellent - You're in great shape for most loans
  • 36-43%: Good - Most lenders will approve you
  • 43-50%: Fair - You may qualify but with higher rates
  • Above 50%: High - Most lenders will decline
  • Front-End DTI: Housing costs only, should be under 28%
  • Back-End DTI: All debts, typically capped at 43% for qualified mortgages
