Understanding Closing Costs

Closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the loan amount and include various fees charged by lenders, title companies, and government agencies.

Common Closing Costs

  • Origination Fee: Lender's fee for processing the loan (0.5-1% of loan)
  • Appraisal Fee: Cost to have the property professionally appraised
  • Title Insurance: Protects against ownership disputes
  • Escrow Fees: Third-party handling of transaction documents
  • Prepaid Items: Property taxes and insurance paid in advance
  • Recording Fees: Government fees to record the deed
