About Roth IRA

A Roth IRA is a retirement account where contributions are made with after-tax dollars, but qualified withdrawals in retirement are completely tax-free.

  • 2024 Contribution Limit: $7,000 ($8,000 if age 50+)
  • Income Limits (2024): Phase-out starts at $146,000 (single) or $230,000 (married)
  • Tax-Free Growth: All earnings grow tax-free and withdrawals are tax-free after 59½
  • No RMDs: Unlike Traditional IRAs, Roth IRAs have no required minimum distributions
  • 5-Year Rule: Account must be open 5 years for tax-free earnings withdrawal
