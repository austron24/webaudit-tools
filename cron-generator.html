Cron Expression Format

Field Values Special Characters
Minute 0-59 * , - /
Hour 0-23 * , - /
Day of Month 1-31 * , - / L W
Month 1-12 * , - /
Day of Week 0-6 (Sun=0) * , - / L #

* = any value  |  , = value list  |  - = range  |  / = step  |  L = last  |  W = weekday
