📖 Caffeine Guide

Safe Limits:
• Adults: Up to 400mg/day (~4 cups coffee)
• Pregnant: Max 200mg/day
• Teens: Max 100mg/day
Sleep Tip: Stop caffeine 6-8 hours before bedtime for better sleep quality.
