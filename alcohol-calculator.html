📊 BAC Effects

0.00 - 0.02%: Little to no effect. Normal behavior.
0.02 - 0.05%: Mild relaxation, slight mood elevation.
0.05 - 0.08%: Reduced coordination, impaired judgment.
0.08 - 0.15%: Legal limit. Significant impairment.
0.15%+: Severe impairment. High risk. Seek help.
⚠️ Disclaimer: This is an estimate only. Many factors affect BAC. Never drink and drive. When in doubt, don't drive.
