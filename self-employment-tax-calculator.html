About Self-Employment Tax

Self-employment tax is the Social Security and Medicare tax for self-employed individuals. It's the equivalent of FICA taxes that employers and employees pay.

  • Total SE tax rate: 15.3% (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare)
  • Social Security wage base (2024): $168,600 - only income up to this limit is subject to SS tax
  • Additional Medicare Tax: 0.9% on income over $200,000 (single) or $250,000 (married)
  • Tax deduction: You can deduct 50% of SE tax on Form 1040
  • Quarterly payments: SE tax is typically paid in quarterly estimated payments
