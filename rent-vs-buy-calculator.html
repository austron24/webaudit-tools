Rent vs Buy: Key Factors

The decision to rent or buy depends on many factors beyond monthly payments.

Consider These Factors

  • Time Horizon: Buying typically makes more sense if you'll stay 5+ years
  • Opportunity Cost: Money for a down payment could be invested elsewhere
  • Hidden Costs: Maintenance, repairs, and HOA fees add up
  • Tax Benefits: Mortgage interest may be tax-deductible
  • Market Conditions: Home appreciation varies by location
  • Flexibility: Renting offers more mobility for career changes
