The Power of Compound Interest

Albert Einstein reportedly called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world." Here's why:

  • Interest on Interest: You earn returns on your previous returns
  • Time is Key: The longer you invest, the more powerful compounding becomes
  • Start Early: Starting 10 years earlier can double your final balance
  • Regular Contributions: Small monthly additions add up significantly
  • Rule of 72: Divide 72 by your rate to estimate years to double money
