Understanding Compound Interest

Compound interest is often called the "eighth wonder of the world" because of its powerful wealth-building effect. Unlike simple interest, compound interest earns interest on your interest.

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) + PMT × [((1 + r/n)^(nt) - 1) / (r/n)]

Where:

  • A = Final amount
  • P = Principal (initial investment)
  • r = Annual interest rate (decimal)
  • n = Number of times compounded per year
  • t = Time in years
  • PMT = Regular contribution amount

Tips for Maximizing Growth

  • Start early - Time is your biggest ally with compound interest
  • Contribute regularly - Consistent contributions accelerate growth
  • Reinvest dividends - Let your earnings compound
  • Minimize fees - High fees eat into your returns
