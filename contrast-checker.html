WCAG Guidelines Explained

Level AA (Minimum)

Normal text: 4.5:1 ratio
Large text (24px+ or 18px+ bold): 3:1 ratio

Level AAA (Enhanced)

Normal text: 7:1 ratio
Large text (24px+ or 18px+ bold): 4.5:1 ratio

Why It Matters

300 million people have color vision deficiency. Low contrast text is hard to read for everyone.

Legal Risk

ADA lawsuits often cite contrast issues. WCAG AA is the legal standard for web accessibility.
