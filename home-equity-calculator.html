Understanding Home Equity

Home equity is the difference between your home's market value and what you owe on it. It builds over time through mortgage payments and home appreciation.

Ways to Access Your Equity

  • Home Equity Loan: Fixed-rate lump sum, typically 80-85% LTV
  • HELOC: Revolving credit line, draw as needed
  • Cash-Out Refinance: Replace mortgage with larger loan
  • Reverse Mortgage: For homeowners 62+ to access equity
