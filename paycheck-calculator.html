Understanding Your Paycheck

Your paycheck includes several mandatory deductions:

  • Federal Income Tax: Based on your income and filing status
  • Social Security: 6.2% of gross pay (up to $160,200 in 2023)
  • Medicare: 1.45% of all earnings
  • State & Local Taxes: Varies by location
  • Pre-tax Deductions: Reduce taxable income (401k, health insurance)
