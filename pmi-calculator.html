How to Remove PMI

1. Automatic Removal (78% LTV)

Lenders must automatically cancel PMI when your loan balance reaches 78% of the original home value.

2. Request Removal (80% LTV)

You can request PMI cancellation when you reach 80% LTV. May require good payment history and home appraisal.

3. Refinance

If your home has appreciated, refinancing can eliminate PMI if your new loan is under 80% of current value.

4. Extra Payments

Make extra principal payments to reach 80% LTV faster.

Understanding PMI

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) protects the lender if you default on your loan. It's required when your down payment is less than 20% of the home's value.

PMI Rate Factors

  • Credit Score: Higher scores = lower PMI rates
  • Down Payment: Larger down payments reduce PMI
  • Loan Type: Fixed vs adjustable rates affect PMI
  • Loan Amount: Higher loans may have higher rates

PMI vs MIP

PMI is for conventional loans. FHA loans have Mortgage Insurance Premium (MIP), which works differently and often lasts the life of the loan.
