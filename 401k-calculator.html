401(k) Contribution Tips

Maximize your retirement savings with these strategies:

  • Get the Full Match: Contribute at least enough to get the full employer match - it's free money!
  • 2024 Limits: You can contribute up to $23,000 ($30,500 if 50+)
  • Increase Gradually: Raise contributions 1% each year to painlessly grow savings
  • Consider Roth 401(k): Pay taxes now for tax-free withdrawals in retirement
  • Check Fees: Low-cost index funds outperform most actively managed funds
