About Your IP Address

Your IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique number assigned by your Internet Service Provider. It identifies your device on the internet and enables communication with websites and services.

IPv4 addresses are 32-bit numbers like 192.168.1.1. IPv6 addresses are 128-bit and look like 2001:0db8:85a3:0000:0000:8a2e:0370:7334.

Your IP can reveal your approximate location, ISP, and network type. For privacy, consider using a VPN to mask your real IP address.
