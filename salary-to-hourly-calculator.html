How to Calculate Hourly Rate from Salary

The basic formula is simple: Annual Salary ÷ (Hours/Week × Weeks/Year)

For a $65,000 salary working 40 hours/week for 52 weeks: $65,000 ÷ 2,080 = $31.25/hour

Consider Your True Hourly Rate

Your true compensation often includes more than base salary:

  • Health insurance - Employer contributions average $7,000-15,000/year
  • 401(k) match - Typically 3-6% of salary
  • Paid time off - Vacation, sick days, and holidays
  • Bonuses - Performance or annual bonuses
  • Stock options - Equity compensation at some companies
