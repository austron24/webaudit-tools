💡 Factors Affecting HVAC Size

🌡️
Climate Zone
Hotter climates need larger AC units; colder climates need more heating capacity.
🏠
Insulation
Better insulation means smaller HVAC needs due to less heat transfer.
🪟
Windows
More windows = more heat gain/loss, requiring larger capacity.
☀️
Sun Exposure
Direct sunlight adds heat load; shaded homes need less cooling.
