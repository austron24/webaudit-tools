Credit Card Payoff Strategies

Paying more than the minimum can dramatically reduce your debt payoff time.

  • Avalanche Method: Pay highest interest rate cards first
  • Snowball Method: Pay smallest balances first for quick wins
  • Balance Transfer: Move debt to a 0% APR card temporarily
  • Debt Consolidation: Combine debts into one lower-rate loan
  • Extra Payments: Even small extra amounts make a big difference
