Understanding Interest-Only Mortgages

Interest-only mortgages allow you to pay only the interest for a set period, typically 5-10 years. After this period, payments increase significantly as you begin paying principal.

How IO Loans Work

  • IO Period: Pay only interest, building no equity
  • After IO: Payments jump as principal is amortized over remaining term
  • Payment Shock: Increase can be 30-50% or more
  • Full Balance Due: Principal still owed at end of IO period

When IO Loans Make Sense

  • Investors expecting property appreciation
  • Variable income (bonuses, commissions)
  • Short-term ownership plans
  • Strategic cash flow management
