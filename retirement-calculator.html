Retirement Planning Tips

Start early and stay consistent to maximize your retirement savings.

  • 4% Rule: Plan to withdraw 4% of savings annually in retirement
  • Employer Match: Always contribute enough to get full employer 401(k) match
  • Diversification: Spread investments across stocks, bonds, and other assets
  • Catch-Up Contributions: After 50, you can contribute extra to retirement accounts
  • Healthcare Costs: Plan for significant healthcare expenses in retirement
