Strategies to Pay Off Faster

Every extra dollar you pay goes directly to principal, reducing both your balance and future interest charges.

Effective Methods

  • Biweekly payments: Make half payments every two weeks (= 13 monthly payments/year)
  • Round up: Round your payment up to the nearest $100
  • Annual bonus: Apply tax refunds or bonuses to principal
  • Refinance to shorter term: 15-year loans have lower rates and faster payoff
