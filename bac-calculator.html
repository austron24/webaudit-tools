📈 BAC Level Guide

0.00-0.02%: Minimal effects
0.03-0.05%: Mild relaxation, slight impairment
0.06-0.08%: Impaired judgment (near legal limit)
0.08%+: Legally impaired - DO NOT DRIVE
0.15%+: Severe impairment, risk of blackout

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This calculator provides estimates only. Actual BAC varies based on many factors including food intake, medications, metabolism, and tolerance. Never drive after drinking. When in doubt, use a ride service or designated driver.
