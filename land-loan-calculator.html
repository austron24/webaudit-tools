📖 Land Loan Information

Higher Rates: Land loans typically have higher interest rates (7-10%+) than mortgages because land is considered a riskier investment.
Larger Down Payments: Expect 20-50% down payment requirements for land loans, especially for raw land.
Shorter Terms: Land loans often have shorter terms (10-20 years) compared to traditional mortgages.
Loan Types:
• Improved Lot: Land with utilities - easier financing
• Raw Land: No improvements - higher rates, larger down payment
• USDA Loans: May be available for farm/rural land
