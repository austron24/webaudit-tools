Understanding Land Loans

Land loans are different from traditional mortgages. Lenders consider them riskier because there's no structure to use as collateral, so terms are typically less favorable.

Types of Land Loans

  • Raw Land Loan - For undeveloped land with no utilities or road access (highest rates, largest down payment)
  • Lot Loan - For improved lots in developed areas with utilities available
  • Construction Loan - Combines land purchase with construction financing
  • USDA Loan - For rural agricultural land (lower rates for qualified buyers)

Typical Requirements

  • 20-50% down payment (higher for raw land)
  • Interest rates 1-3% higher than home mortgages
  • Shorter terms (5-20 years typical)
  • Good credit score (680+ preferred)
  • Clear development plans help approval
