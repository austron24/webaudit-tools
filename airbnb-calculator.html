Airbnb Hosting Guide

Short-term rentals can be lucrative but require careful analysis. Occupancy rates and nightly rates vary significantly by location and season.

Key Metrics to Track

  • Occupancy Rate - 50-70% is typical; top listings reach 80%+
  • Average Daily Rate (ADR) - Compare to hotels and competitors
  • RevPAR - Revenue per available night (ADR × Occupancy)
  • Cash-on-Cash Return - Annual cash flow ÷ cash invested

Hidden Costs to Consider

  • Higher turnover means more wear and tear
  • Linens, towels, and consumables need regular replacement
  • Short-term rental insurance costs more than standard
  • Local permits and taxes may apply
  • Guest communication and key exchanges take time
