About Hash Functions

Hash functions convert data into fixed-length strings. MD5 (128-bit) is fast but not cryptographically secure. SHA-1 (160-bit) is also deprecated for security use. SHA-256 and SHA-512 (256/512-bit) are part of SHA-2 family and are secure for cryptographic purposes. All processing happens in your browser - no data is sent to any server.
