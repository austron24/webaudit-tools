💡 About Sleep Cycles

A typical sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes. Waking up at the end of a cycle (rather than in the middle) helps you feel more refreshed. Adults need 5-6 cycles (7.5-9 hours) per night for optimal health.

📊 Sleep Recommendations by Age

👶 Newborns (0-3 months)14-17 hours
🧒 Toddlers (1-2 years)11-14 hours
👦 Children (6-13 years)9-11 hours
🧑 Teens (14-17 years)8-10 hours
👨 Adults (18-64 years)7-9 hours
👴 Seniors (65+ years)7-8 hours
